Thanksgiving Guests Want In on Your AI Plan: Five Plans, Priced Per Person

Fourteen at the table, three who want a login, and a price list that changes shape depending on whether the plan was built for one person or for a house.

Three of your fourteen guests will ask to use your ChatGPT, and two of them will ask on Thursday morning with a plate already in one hand. Handing over the phone costs nothing. Three proper seats cost $60 a month. Google AI Pro covers six people for $19.99, in the same month, on the same card.

That gap is the decision. Not the $40. The thing you are actually buying with the $40 is separation: six accounts instead of one login passed around a kitchen, so your search history does not end up holding somebody else's flight times.

The table below prices five consumer plans at three people rather than one, because three is the number a holiday table tends to produce. Every price is the vendor's own, read on November 17, 2026.

Two rows behave differently from the rest. Google AI Pro and Microsoft 365 Family are household products, so the per-person number falls as the house fills: Google lands at $3.33 a head with six people on the plan, Microsoft at $1.39. The other three are single-person licenses. The cost per person is the price on the page, and three people cost three times as much.

At three people the twelve-month arithmetic is blunt. Three ChatGPT Plus seats: $720. Google AI Pro for the same year: $239.88. The difference is $480.12, or 66.7% of the three-seat bill. Microsoft 365 Family runs $99.99 a year for six people, which is $33.33 per person for the year if three of you use it.

Why the Household Plans Win on More Than Price

Each family member signs in as themselves. Their Gemini chats, their Drive files, their saved facts and their search history live in their own account, and yours stay in yours. That is the part worth paying for, and it is the part a shared password cannot imitate at any price.

Two limits matter before Thursday. A Google family group holds six people total, you included, and membership is not a revolving door: the group can be changed roughly once every twelve months, so you cannot cycle four cousins through it across a weekend. Microsoft 365 Family also seats six, gives each person 1 TB of OneDrive, and includes Copilot inside the desktop Word, Excel and Outlook apps.

Both products are also not the same thing as the $20 chat subscription. Google AI Pro is Gemini plus 2 TB of storage plus the Workspace integrations. Microsoft's plan is Office plus storage plus Copilot in those apps. If the guest's actual request is "let me ask follow-up questions about my resume," either one handles it. If it is "I want the thing you use," they will notice the difference in about ten minutes.

Team Plans Are the Wrong Tool for Three People

OpenAI and Anthropic both sell seats, and both put a floor under the order. ChatGPT Team bills $25 a seat on annual billing with a two-seat minimum, so three people cost $75 a month against $60 for three individual Plus plans. Claude Team has a five-seat minimum at $25 a seat: $125 a month for a household of three, which is $65 more than three Pro plans and $780 more across a year.

Those plans buy admin controls, central billing, SSO and a data-handling arrangement written for companies. None of that is a Thanksgiving problem. Do not buy a seat minimum to solve a guest problem; you will pay for seats nobody sits in.

What You Give Away With a Shared Login

Four things, roughly in the order they will irritate you.

  1. Your history. Every question a guest types appears in your sidebar under your name, and the follow-ups they ask about your sister's divorce stay there after they hand the phone back.
  2. Your memory. All three assistants save facts between conversations now. A guest's question can be saved as a fact about you, and the next conversation you open will start from it.
  3. Your instructions. Custom instructions are set once per account and applied to every conversation, including the one your brother opens at 11 p.m.
  4. Your card. Anything bought inside the app bills the payment method on file, and there is no second approval step between a guest and your balance.

The terms point the same way. OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity license consumer accounts to one person, and the household option is the family or team product rather than a shared password. I have never seen a vendor enforce this against a family at Thanksgiving. That is not the argument. The argument is that the account stops being yours the moment two people write into the same history.

What to Do Before Wednesday Night

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26 this year and Black Friday opens the next morning, so the decision window closes on Wednesday evening rather than on the day.

  1. Count who will actually use it. At a table of fourteen the honest number is two or three, usually the ones who already pay for something. The other eleven want to watch a video.
  2. Three or more: buy a household plan. Google AI Pro at $19.99 if they live in Gmail and Docs, Microsoft 365 Family at $99.99 a year if they need Word and Excel, and either one if the request is only chat.
  3. One person: change nothing. Keep your single plan and point the guest at the free tier on their own phone. It handles a weekend of questions, and the answers stay on their device.
  4. Set the family group up before they arrive. Invitations, acceptance and the first sign-in on a guest's own phone take longer than you think once the doorbell starts.
  5. Skip the seat-minimum plans. A five-seat minimum is a business purchase, and the Black Friday payback math only makes sense on plans you would have bought anyway.

The Verdict

Google AI Pro is the pick for a mixed household, and the reason is the six separate accounts rather than the $19.99. Three people on their own logins, one bill, $480.12 saved against three Plus seats over a year, and nobody's history mixing with anybody else's.

Buy Microsoft 365 Family instead if the three of you open Word or Excel more than once a month, because the Office apps are the actual product and Copilot rides along. Keep a single ChatGPT Plus if you are the only one who will use it, and read what the free tier stops doing before you assume you need the $20 at all.

Claude is the one to buy one seat at a time. Pro at $20 is a fair price for a single writer and a bad deal as a household plan, given the five-seat floor on Team. For the rest of the weekend, the cost per usable hour is the number that decides whether any of this was worth it, and the renewal date is the one you will meet again next November.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I just give my brother my ChatGPT login for the weekend?

It works technically and it is not what the account is licensed for. Consumer plans at OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity are sold to one person, and the household option is a separate product. The practical cost shows up on Sunday: his questions sit in your history, and anything he says that looks like a fact about you can be saved to memory and carried into your next conversation.

Does Google AI Pro let me share Gemini with my family?

Yes. A Google AI Pro plan can be shared with up to five other people through a family group, six accounts in total, and each person signs in as themselves. The plan is $19.99 a month as of November 2026. Group membership is the constraint: Google limits how often a family group can change, roughly once every twelve months, so plan who is in it before you send the invitations.

Is Microsoft 365 Family cheaper than three ChatGPT Plus subscriptions?

On paper, far cheaper: $99.99 a year for six people against $720 a year for three Plus seats. They are not the same purchase. Microsoft's plan is Word, Excel, Outlook, 1 TB of OneDrive per person and Copilot inside those desktop apps. If the reason you wanted Plus was long conversations in a chat window, compare it against Google AI Pro before you switch.

Is Claude Team worth it for three people?

No. Claude Team has a five-seat minimum at $25 a seat on annual billing, so the smallest order is $125 a month, against $60 for three individual Pro plans. That is $780 more over a year for admin controls a household does not use. Buy Pro seats one at a time and keep the five-seat tier for a company that needs central billing.
