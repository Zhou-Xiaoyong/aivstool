Three Questions Dressed As One

A cancellation feels like one problem. It is three. Can I still get there? What does the airline owe me? What do I say to the person who finally answers?

The first has one honest answer, and it is not a chatbot. Airlines hold their own inventory and release it to their own rebooking screen first, so a seat visible in the app at 6:20 may not exist anywhere else until 6:40. None of the three tools has that feed. Any of them claiming to check availability is describing what a web page said, not what is in the cabin.

The other two are policy text and phone scripts, which is where a $20 subscription earns its month.

What the Airline Owes You

The rule is federal, it is short, and it is the part most passengers get wrong. Under the DOT's automatic refund rule, a canceled flight means a refund in cash to the original form of payment if you do not accept the rebooking or a voucher. No form to fill in, no negotiating at a counter. Seven business days for a card, 20 calendar days for anything else.

Two details decide how the morning goes. A voucher is not a refund, and an airline may only substitute one if you agree to it. And the entitlement ends the moment you accept the rebooked flight, which is why the order matters more than any prompt you can write.

The same rule calls a domestic flight significantly changed when it departs three or more hours early or arrives three or more hours late. Hold that number when the app offers a 4:50 p.m. connection that lands at 11 p.m. Offering it is legal. Taking the refund and driving is also legal, and on the Wednesday before Thanksgiving the drive is often the better trade.

They are the part that stops you accepting a bad offer because you have been awake since five. Call while the app is open, not after: the app can hold a seat the phone agent cannot see for another twenty minutes, and the agent can waive what the app will not offer.

If It Is Already Too Late

If you already tapped accept on a rebooking that lands after dinner, the refund is gone, and arguing about it wastes the one hour you still control. The trip is fixable. Ask the desk about the same-day change list, which most large carriers sell even against a canceled ticket, and ask about a hotel if the new flight leaves tomorrow.

If you checked a bag and then did not fly, the bag is a separate claim. The rule covers the checked bag fee when the bag is not at the gate within 12 hours of a domestic arrival, and the clock starts at the arrival time, not the moment you notice. File the report before you leave the airport.

The Verdict

Perplexity won the twenty minutes: two hand-fixes, a quoted notice, three real flights, and the 3-hour threshold without being asked for it. Its weakness is easy to work around: it cites the web, so read every flight number back into the app before repeating it to an agent.

ChatGPT won the money question. If your only problem is what you are owed and what to say, its answer was cleaner and the script needed no editing, which matters when you have 90 seconds of an agent's attention. The wall at message 9 is the argument for keeping the free tab for policy and paying for live search, not the other way around.

Gemini is the one I would not use for a live disruption. The weather mistake was invisible, phrased with the same confidence as the correct parts, and at a counter that difference is a hotel room. It is fine on stable ground: our 355-mile route test put it within a few minutes of ChatGPT, and the tech support tests show the same shape on smaller problems, right up until the situation changes under it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can ChatGPT rebook a canceled flight?

No. ChatGPT has no airline inventory feed and no booking authority, so it cannot hold, change or confirm a seat. In my test it did not even know the flight had been canceled; it told me to check the airline app. What it can do is tell you what the carrier owes you under the DOT rule and write a phone script you can read to an agent.

What does the airline owe me when it cancels my flight?

Under the DOT's automatic refund rule, a refund in cash to your original form of payment if you do not accept the rebooking or a voucher. It has to be automatic rather than requested, and it has to arrive within seven business days for a credit card payment or 20 calendar days for anything else. The entitlement ends if you accept the rebooking.

Does the 24-hour cancellation rule cover a canceled flight?

No, that is a different rule. The 24-hour rule protects a ticket you just bought and then changed your mind about, as long as the booking was made at least a week before departure. When the airline cancels, the automatic refund rule applies instead, and it is stronger: no request, no fee, cash rather than credit.

Which AI is best for a flight disruption?

Perplexity, for the first twenty minutes, because it reads the carrier's live notice and names alternatives with times. ChatGPT writes the better phone script and states the refund rule more precisely. Neither can rebook, so the airline app stays open the whole time. Gemini was the only one of the three that assigned the wrong cause for the cancellation, which matters because the cause decides the hotel.

My flight leaves in 90 minutes. What should I actually do?

Skip the chatbot and open the airline app, then call the elite line while the app is loading. Accept a rebooking only if the alternative arrives within three hours of your original arrival time; beyond that, take the refund and drive rather than trade a dinner for a middle seat at midnight. Use AI later, for a hotel claim or a bag report.
