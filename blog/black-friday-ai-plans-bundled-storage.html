Black Friday AI Plans 2026: The $20 Tier That Already Includes Your Storage

Seven plans priced twice: once as advertised, once with the storage or Office apps subtracted at the price the vendor charges for them separately.

If you already pay $9.99 a month for 2 TB of cloud storage, Google AI Pro costs you $10.00 a month, not $19.99. The plan ships with the same 2 TB, and Google sells that storage by itself for $9.99.

I read the pricing pages for seven consumer AI plans on November 18, 2026, nine days before Black Friday: Google AI Pro, ChatGPT Plus, ChatGPT Go, Claude Pro, Perplexity Pro, Microsoft 365 Personal and Microsoft 365 Family. Not one of them lists a Black Friday discount. Three of them list a bundle, and the bundle is the only place where the advertised price and the real price come apart.

So the table below prices every plan twice, once as the vendor advertises it and once with the storage or the Office apps subtracted, at the price the vendor charges for that piece on its own page.

Where the Subtraction Stops Working

Three conditions, and the $10.00 figure collapses if any one of them fails.

You have to need the 2 TB. If your photos and documents fit inside the free 15 GB, you are not paying $9.99 for storage, so nothing is being replaced and Google charges you the full $19.99 for the model. The subtraction is an accounting entry, not a discount, and it only pays out against a bill you actually receive.

You have to be willing to leave the model. The saving exists only on the way out of ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro. The prompts you tuned, the projects you saved and the custom instructions you wrote live in the account you are closing. Most people who switch for the storage math switch back inside a month. Saving $9.99 while rebuilding a year of workflow is a bad trade, and I would not recommend it to anyone whose work runs through one of those two models.

Microsoft's family plan is not a household AI plan. Microsoft 365 Family covers six people for $129.99 a year, and the comparison table on Microsoft's own buy page carries a line worth reading twice: AI for subscription owner only. Six accounts get Word, Excel and Outlook. One account, the owner's, gets the advanced Copilot features. The 2 TB is shared across the household rather than handed out per person. If three people in the house want an assistant, this plan serves one of them, and the AI half of the bundle belongs to whoever bought it.

What a Year Locks In

Nothing above changes on November 27. The annual option is where the only real difference sits, and it is available on any Tuesday.

Claude Pro bills $20 a month or $200 a year, which is $40 less over twelve months and $16.67 a month across the term. Microsoft 365 Family is $12.99 a month or $129.99 a year, a saving of $25.89, about 16.6%. Microsoft 365 Personal is $99.99 a year against $9.99 a month, which saves $19.89 over the term, and Microsoft is currently running the first year at $69.99. Office Home 2024, the one-time copy with no Copilot and no storage, is $179.99.

Google AI Pro and ChatGPT Plus are month to month on their US pricing pages this week. Nothing to commit to and nothing to lose by waiting, which is why I would run the free tier for two weeks before paying for either. If you want the annual question answered plan by plan, the year-up-front payback table walks it line by line.

Which One to Buy on Friday

Paying for storage and living in Gmail or Docs: Google AI Pro. $19.99, $10.00 net, and a bill you were already paying disappears from the card. Gemini turns up inside Docs, Gmail and Vids, which is the difference between a chatbot in a tab and a model working inside the file you have open. The head-to-head against Plus covers where it loses.

Doing work that needs the reasoning tier: ChatGPT Plus. $20, no bundle, no apology needed. Codex, projects and the higher upload limits are the reason the plan exists. Keep your storage where it is. $29.99 for the plan you need costs less than $20 for the plan you abandon in a month. If longer chats and more images are the whole ask, Go covers it for $8 with ads. What the free tier stops doing is the number that decides that one.

Writing for a living: Claude Pro, annual. $200 up front against $240 monthly, and the fourteen-day comparison is the place to check whether Pro earns it before you commit. Read the refund window first, because annual billing is the one purchase in this group you cannot walk back in a week.

Running Word and Excel all day: Microsoft 365 Personal. $99.99 a year, $8.33 a month, 1 TB and Copilot inside the desktop apps. Buy Family at $129.99 only if other people in the house need the Office apps too, and read the owner-only line above before assuming they get the assistant as well.

The Verdict

Google AI Pro is the plan to buy this Black Friday if you already pay for cloud storage. At $10.00 a month after the subtraction it is the cheapest net price for a frontier model in this group, and it is the only bundle here that removes a bill instead of adding a feature.

The boundary is the model, not the price. If your work depends on ChatGPT's reasoning tier or Claude's writing, the $9.99 you save costs you the workflow you built on top of it, and you should pay the sticker price without pretending the bundle math applies. Bundles reward people whose subscriptions overlap. They punish people who switch tools to chase a line item, which is the mistake this table exists to prevent. For the rest of the week, what each plan costs per usable hour is the honest denominator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Google AI Pro really include 2 TB of storage?

Yes. Google AI Pro is $19.99 a month as of November 2026 and includes 2 TB of Google One storage that can be shared with up to five family members, plus Gemini inside Gmail, Docs and Vids. Google sells 2 TB without any AI for $9.99 a month, which is where the $10.00 net figure in this article comes from.

Is there a Black Friday discount on ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro in 2026?

Neither vendor listed a Black Friday discount on its US pricing page on November 18, 2026. The only price difference available is annual billing, and Claude Pro offers it at $200 a year against $240 paid monthly. OpenAI's cheaper option is ChatGPT Go at $8 a month, which is a different plan rather than a sale on Plus.

Does everyone on Microsoft 365 Family get Copilot?

No. Six accounts receive Word, Excel and Outlook, but advanced Copilot features go to the subscription owner only, which Microsoft states on its own plan comparison page. The 2 TB of OneDrive is also shared across the household rather than allocated per person, so a family of six does not get six separate terabytes.

Is ChatGPT Go at $8 enough instead of Plus?

Go gives you the fast model with ten times the free plan's messages, uploads and image creation, plus longer memory, and OpenAI has said it will test ads on it in the US. It does not carry the reasoning models, projects, scheduled tasks or the Codex allowance that Plus includes. If your work needs any of those, Go is a stopgap rather than a substitute.
