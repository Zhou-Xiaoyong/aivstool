Best AI Tools for Holiday Ad Creative in 2026

Three stills, one 12-second spot, eight days to a deadline: what each plan charges per usable asset.

A two-location bakery needs three vertical stills and one twelve-second spot in the ad account by November 18, eight days before Thanksgiving. Three plans cover the whole brief for $31 a month, and the surprise is which one did the cheap work.

The stills cost 28 cents each on a $10 plan and $1.73 each on a $15 plan. The $15 plan still earned its money, because it was the only one of the three that could shoot the part that moves at 1080 by 1920.

We ran the brief on November 5 and 6, 2026, on paid accounts, and wrote down every generation, every credit and every minute. Prices below come from the three vendors' own pricing pages, read on November 7, 2026.

Bottom line: Midjourney Basic at $10 for the stills, Runway Standard at $15 for the motion, ElevenLabs Starter at $6 if the spot needs a voice. $31 a month, against $17.14 of quota actually consumed. Do not sign an annual plan before you have kept the tool ten months, and do not read Runway's 25%-off Kling promotion as a price cut on the subscription: it discounts generations, it ends November 30, and by then your campaign has already shipped.

Your Deadline Is November 18, Not November 26

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26, 2026. Nobody buys a pre-order on the day. The promotion has to be live by Friday the 20th, and paid placements need a day or two of review before the spend starts, so the files are due on the 18th. That leaves eleven days from today and about eight of them for production, because sign-off eats the rest.

If you expect two campaigns a month rather than one, the answer is Runway Pro at $35 for 2,250 credits, and the arithmetic changes: Pro costs 2.3 times Standard and carries 3.6 times the allowance.

One production note nobody puts in a pricing table. Neither image tool set the promo price correctly in the frame, so the "$24 pre-order" line was composited afterward in HTML and exported. Budget thirty minutes for type and you will not lose an afternoon to a bakery sign with a melted "4".

Where the Free Tiers Stop

Every one of these tools will generate something for nothing. None of them will let you run it as an ad.

Midjourney. General commercial terms are listed as a feature of the paid plans, from Basic at $10 upward. The free trial is for looking, not for shipping.

ElevenLabs. The pricing page puts the Commercial License in the Starter column, one row under "everything in free". Starter is $1 for the first month and $6 after that. Ten thousand free credits a month will read your script, and the license that makes the read usable in a paid placement is the $6.

Runway. Free is 125 credits, granted once. Watermark-free export is a Standard feature at $15, and an ad with a watermark is not an ad.

So the honest floor for this brief is $25 without a voiceover and $31 with one. Anyone quoting $0 is quoting a draft you cannot legally run. Our roundup of free AI tools draws that line in more places.

What to Buy Now, and What to Wait On

The 20% annual discount pays back in month ten

Both image vendors price their annual plan at 20% off: Midjourney Basic is $8 a month billed yearly, $96 up front, against $10 monthly. Runway Standard is $12 a month billed yearly, $144 up front, against $15. Divide the up-front total by the monthly price and both land on 9.6 months. You have to keep the plan into month ten before the discount beats paying month to month, and in November you are guessing about July.

For a five-week campaign the answer is monthly, every time. The same holds higher up the ladders, and it is why our Claude Pro annual calculation uses the same division rather than the marketing percentage.

The first-month prices are a trap with a calendar

ElevenLabs sells Starter at $1 for the first month and Creator at $11, then $6 and $22. That is a real discount on a real invoice, and it expires thirty days after you claim it. Buy it on November 10 for a November 18 deadline and the cheap month covers the whole job. Buy it today for a January campaign and you have spent the discount before you have a script.

Skip the middle tiers this month

Midjourney Standard at $30 adds HD video and fifteen hours of Fast generation. If the motion work is going to Runway, that is $20 for a feature you will not open. ElevenLabs Creator at $22 quadruples the credits to 121,000, and this brief used 800. Neither is wrong in February. Both are wrong eleven days before a deadline.

The Verdict

Winner for stills: Midjourney Basic, $10. Three usable stills from 17 jobs, 28 cents each, and the only tool in the test that produced all three without a reshoot.

Winner for motion: Runway Standard, $15. One finished twelve-second cut from two finals and eight drafts, no watermark, $12.67 of quota. The $25 pair is also cheaper than Runway Pro at $35, and it beats Runway alone by 6.1 times on the cost of a still.

Boundary: if you need one invoice, one brand kit and three spots a month, buy Runway Pro at $35 and stop assembling a stack. The economics flip the moment the production volume is monthly rather than seasonal.

Skip list: Midjourney Standard, ElevenLabs Creator, and any annual plan signed before you have kept the tool ten months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I produce a holiday ad entirely on free tiers?

Not one you can run as a paid placement. Midjourney lists general commercial terms as a feature of its paid plans, ElevenLabs puts the commercial license in the Starter column at $6, and Runway's watermark-free export starts on Standard at $15. The free tiers are good for pitching an idea to the owner. The moment the file goes into an ad account, one of those three thresholds has to be paid.

Is the Runway Kling 25% discount a subscription discount?

No. It is 25% off Kling 4.0 generations until November 30, 2026, and Kling 4.0 at 1080p costs 118 credits for five seconds before the discount. It reduces the cost of generating clips, not the monthly price of the plan, and it ends after a Thanksgiving campaign has already shipped. Treat it as a reason to keep the plan open through December.

How long does a twelve-second spot take to produce?

Three hours and thirty-seven minutes of hands-on work across two evenings in our test: 58 minutes on stills, 106 minutes on motion including eight drafts and two finals, 19 minutes on the voiceover and 34 minutes assembling the cut with the price line set as type. Add two rounds of owner feedback, which is calendar time rather than work time.

Do I need an annual plan for a five-week campaign?

No. A 20% annual discount pays back in month 9.6, so you have to keep the plan into month ten to come out ahead. Midjourney Basic is $96 up front against $120 paid monthly, and Runway Standard is $144 against $180. A five-week campaign is 1.2 months. Buy monthly, and revisit in February when you know whether the tool survived the winter.
