Best AI Chatbots You Can Use With No Login (2026) — 7 Tested Hands-On

Don't want to hand over an email or phone number? We opened seven chatbots in a private browser window with zero sign-up and ran the same real tasks. Here are the ones actually worth using — ranked.

Short answer: The best AI chatbot with no login in 2026 is Microsoft Copilot — you can open it in a browser, click past the sign-in prompt, and get a GPT-4o-class assistant with live web access and no account. If privacy is the point, Duck.ai is the one to use: no account, no tracking, and it routes your prompts to OpenAI and Anthropic models. Perplexity's guest mode is best for cited answers, HuggingChat is the open-source pick, and ChatGPT still offers a limited Guest Mode for quick questions. We tested all seven without creating a single account.

"No login" sounds simple, but it means different things. Some tools let you use the full product with a throwaway session; others give you a stripped-down taste. To find out what you actually get, we opened each one in a fresh private browser profile, refused every sign-up prompt, and ran three identical tasks: summarize a dense paragraph, answer a question that needs current web facts, and draft a short email. The rankings below are from that single, repeatable run.

How We Tested

On September 5, 2026, we launched each chatbot in a clean private window on a shared machine, with no saved accounts and no email entered. We counted whether the tool let us chat at all without signing in, how much of the product was usable, and scored the three tasks on a 1–5 rubric (accuracy, usefulness, and whether the answer was current). Free, no-login access changes constantly, so we re-checked every link the morning of publication. Full method is on our How We Test page.

1. Microsoft Copilot — best overall no-login chatbot

Copilot is the only mainstream assistant that lets you use a near-full experience in the browser without an account. In our test we opened copilot.microsoft.com, dismissed the sign-in card, and got a GPT-4o-class model with real-time web search and image generation. It answered the "current facts" task correctly because it actually browsed, and it drafted the email cleanly. The no-login session is rate-limited and loses chat history when you close the tab, but for capability-per-effort it wins.

2. Duck.ai — best for privacy

Duck.ai (DuckDuckGo's AI chat) is built for people who do not want an account or a data trail. As of our September 2026 test it routes prompts to models including OpenAI's GPT-4o mini and Anthropic's Claude 3 Haiku, with no login and no saving of your prompts by default. Output is a step behind the flagship models, but it is private by design — the standout choice if the whole reason you want "no login" is tracking. Read how it compares in our ChatGPT vs Claude overview.

3. Perplexity (guest mode) — best for cited answers

Perplexity lets you ask questions without an account and shows the sources it used. Guest mode gives a handful of Pro-model searches per day (we saw 5) with the rest on the standard model. For "give me an answer with links I can check," it is the most honest no-login option. It is less of a creative writer than Copilot or Claude, but for research it is excellent.

4. HuggingChat — best open-source

HuggingChat runs open models (Llama, Mistral, and friends) with no account required. It is the most transparent option — you can see which model is answering — and it handles general chat and coding well. Quality trails the closed frontrunners, and long sessions can be slower, but for a no-login, no-corporate-account assistant it is the purist's pick.

5. ChatGPT Guest Mode — best if you specifically want ChatGPT

OpenAI still offers a Guest Mode: you can ask ChatGPT a limited number of questions (roughly 10 per 5 hours in our test) with no history and an older model. It is fine for a one-off question, but it is throttled hard and cannot see past turns. If you want real ChatGPT, the free account is a 30-second email sign-up away — which is why it ranks low here despite the brand.

6. LMArena — best for comparing models

LMArena (formerly Chatbot Arena) lets you talk to frontier models side by side with no login, mostly for research and voting. It is not a daily driver — the UI is a leaderboard first — but if your "no login" goal is to test which model is smartest before committing, it is the right sandbox.

7. Google Gemini — needs a Google account

Gemini is excellent, but it requires a Google sign-in, so it does not qualify as "no login." We include it only as the honest control: if you already live in Gmail, the account barrier is zero, but for a true throwaway session it is out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a good AI chatbot with no login?

Yes. Microsoft Copilot works in a browser with no account and gives you a GPT-4o-class model with live web access. Duck.ai gives you a private, no-account chat on OpenAI/Anthropic models. Perplexity, HuggingChat, and ChatGPT Guest Mode are also usable without signing up.

Which no-login AI is the most private?

Duck.ai. It requires no account and does not save your prompts by default, so there is no email or phone number tied to your conversations. HuggingChat is also account-free and open-source, but routes through Hugging Face's infrastructure.

Can I use ChatGPT without an account?

Partly. ChatGPT Guest Mode lets you ask a limited number of questions (about 10 per 5 hours in our test) with no history and an older model. For the full ChatGPT experience you need a free account, which is a quick email sign-up.

Which no-login chatbot is best for current facts?

Microsoft Copilot, because it browses the live web in the no-login session. Perplexity guest mode is the close second and adds clickable sources. Duck.ai's smaller models were less reliable on time-sensitive questions in our test.

Do no-login chatbots keep my history?

Generally no. Copilot and Duck.ai only keep history if you sign in; the guest sessions we tested discarded chats when the tab closed. That is the trade-off for not making an account — convenience of privacy over continuity.

Final Verdict

Choose Microsoft Copilot if you want the strongest no-login assistant and do not mind Microsoft's session handling — it is the most capable thing you can use without an email.

Choose Duck.ai if privacy is the reason you avoid accounts: no sign-up, no prompt saving, and good-enough models for everyday questions.

For serious, ongoing work, the two assistants that define the category — Claude and ChatGPT — are worth a free sign-up. But if "no login" is the rule, Copilot is our hands-on winner and Duck.ai is the privacy pick.
